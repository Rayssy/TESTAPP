Air Algérie Catering

Delivering quality inflight meals and airport services for Air Algérie passengers.

What We Do

Air Algérie Catering specializes in creating and delivering inflight meals, snacks, and beverages for Air Algérie flights. The company manages menu planning, food preparation, and safe transportation of meals to aircraft, ensuring guests receive fresh and well-presented dining options during their journey.

Our Services

  • Inflight catering services for domestic and international flights
  • Customized meal planning and menu development
  • Airport lounge and hospitality support
  • Food safety, quality control, and reliable logistics

Why It Matters

By combining culinary expertise with strict safety standards, Air Algérie Catering enhances the travel experience while supporting the airline's operational needs. The company focuses on reliable service delivery, passenger satisfaction, and consistent meal quality across all flights.
